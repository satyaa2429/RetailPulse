FORECAST READINESS
Building your history
Record your daily sales to prepare your forecast.
0 / 3 dayscomplete days
Today’s sales become a complete day tomorrow.
Early estimates start after 3 complete days. Each item builds its own history; smoothing / Holt-Winters comparison starts at 28 days; Random Forest joins at 90 days.

      
HOW CLOSE WERE THE PREDICTIONS?
Forecast accuracy
Checked on past sales
Available methods: exponential smoothing, Holt-Winters (weekly patterns), and Random Forest. Comparison uses only methods with enough completed history.
Training compares models on earlier dates, then checks them on a separate final period.
MethodAverage error in units ↓Overall error % ↓
Average error (MAE) is the error in units per product per day. Overall error (WAPE) compares total errors with total units sold. Lower is better.
